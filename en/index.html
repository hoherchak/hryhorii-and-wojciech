Good to Know

Important Details

Dress Code

Summer Formal
Suits and ties for men, and dresses for women.

Note: The only access to the ceremony at Cameo Island is via a wooden bridge. High heels are strongly discouraged! Opt for flats or wedges instead.

What to Pack

Greece in September is beautiful and sunny! Don't forget your sunglasses and SPF for the daytime ceremony.

As the sun sets, the Mediterranean breeze can get a bit chilly, so we highly recommend bringing a light jacket or shawl for the evening reception.

Gifts

Discover

Explore Zakynthos

Marathonisi (Turtle Island)

Located just across the bay from our ceremony spot. Take a short boat tour to swim in crystal clear waters and spot the famous Caretta-Caretta sea turtles.

Keri Caves

Close to Cameo Island, these stunning white rock formations are perfect for a day trip of snorkeling, swimming, and exploring the dramatic coastline.

Navagio Beach

The iconic Shipwreck Cove! It's further north, but if you're extending your stay, taking a boat tour or visiting the towering cliffside viewpoint is an absolute must-do.
